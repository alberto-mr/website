On **July 16, 2026**, I was promoted to **Associate Professor** at Politecnico di Torino.

This milestone would not have been possible without the people I have been lucky to work with over the years. I am especially grateful to the [e-Lite research group](https://elite.polito.it/), which has been my scientific home throughout this journey — from the early days as a PhD student to the roles that followed. The group's collaborative spirit, curiosity, and constant support have shaped both my research and the way I approach academic life.

A special thank you goes to **Luigi De Russis** and **Fulvio Corno**, for their guidance, trust, and encouragement over the years. Their mentorship and example have been invaluable, and I am proud to keep contributing to the work we share within e-Lite.

I look forward to the next chapter — continuing to grow with the group, and to pushing our research on digital wellbeing, deceptive designs, and human-computer interaction further ahead.
